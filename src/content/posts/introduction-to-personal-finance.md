After pandemic, we used to live with inflation in many different countries. There’s not an exact economic crisis but inflation rates and life expenses increased exponentially for a couple years. Related to that, it has an effect on people for pushing them for investment and thinking more about their personal finance.

*Personal finance is a type of management, which all the investments and expenditures that an individual makes by considering his/her interests in the light of the knowledge he/she has.*

Thinking about personal finance begins with getting paid. If you have a job and a salary, then you should think about your personal finance. Or if your parents give you any amount of money as an allowance for paying your expenses or a fixed amount to handle it yourself, then you should think about it too.

But other than these factors, nowadays, many countries are in trouble with inflation and rising cost of living. Some countries’ problem are bigger and others are smaller but each of them have this issue. However, it’s not a situation that you cannot exclude yourself. It is highly possible to handle totally different economic situation from your country.

## My Principles of Personal Finance

To apply thinks that I mentioned above to your life, you should be able to make your own decisions about your own economics. There are few basic principles to take your economic situation to upper level and hold it there although the situation around you makes it difficult.

### 📈 Keep Track

Firstly, you should be able to look into your economic situation. There has to be a overview of your expenses, salary, investment portfolio and maybe debt. It is as basic as looking into your bank account and getting an overview about your personal finance.

It is also has benefits to get these information and transform it to some graphs. Month by month, you can measure your total balance, debts, how much money you paid for which expenses etc. to turn these data to something visual.

Someone with irregular income (like a freelancer), as seen below, can easily compare their income every month to see if there is an improvement in their economic situation.

Not just your income, you should record your savings, mortgage debt, life expenses… All of these give you a proper knowledge of “Where I am right now in terms of personal finance?” You can keep asking this to yourself periodically.

### ⏳ Save regularly

You get paid each month. Then you pay your bills, pay credit card debt, give some to charity… Finally, some money remains (we hope so) and its called saving. Don’t let this money to fight (and of course lose) inflation, whatever the amount is just ***invest with it.***

Investing is a deep subject in itself and I will not dive into that subject in this article. However, one of the cornerstones of personal finance is to invest informed. The key is, whether you invest the right thing or wrong, if you invest regularly, at the end of the day, you’ll have done a lot better than having the money in the bank.

### 🧠 Keep Your Investments

If you are not a trader, then you don’t know about candlestick charts, patterns, futures etc. these are the terms that the person doing the job should know about. If you are not a trader, do not make many trades between short periods.

If you want to make consistent investments for the distant future, perhaps it is best not to touch this money until when you need it. Whether you buy gold, silver or stocks of companies or funds, you just don’t have to be in green forever. There will be reds and grays too.

The thing is, being patient and not thinking of yourself as a trader because you are not. You have a saving to save against inflation.
