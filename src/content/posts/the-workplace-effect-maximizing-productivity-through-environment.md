As a software engineer, I always try to be 100% focused during a workday, or on a side project. But anyone can achieve that by staying concentrated all the time. Surely your focus will decrease during your work session, like an ice melting with heat.

![my main work area](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*iAmCN66Xml1JiAycEDsdTA.jpeg)

You can think of your brain state, your focus like an ice cube, no matter how big it is, or how cold it is, it will melt at the end. But there are ways to delay the melting of your ice cube. Think of these ways to protect it from heat like a thermos, you can decrease heat by the way, but it’s easier to control your environment than controlling weather.

Creating an optimal workplace environment is like designing the perfect thermos for your focus ice cube. The right setup can significantly extend the time before your concentration wanes. Factors such as lighting, noise levels, ergonomics, and even the colors of your workspace can either support or sabotage your productivity.

By fine-tuning these elements, you can create a conducive atmosphere that not only preserves your focus longer but also enhances your overall efficiency. Investing time in optimizing your work environment pays off, ensuring that your ice cube of concentration stays solid and effective throughout your workday.

In this post, I will give a few suggestions that I have used myself to protect your focus from external factors.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*3DBUfKjd0Dj_XwqbTLy5Ww.jpeg)

## Ergonomics

No one can focus with pain in their back, wrist, or neck. So if you are planning to build a workspace for yourself, give the highest budget to a mouse, keyboard, and of course, a comfortable seat.

I use a [highly adjustable seat](https://adoremobilya.com/products/techno-file-sirtli-ofis-koltugu-siyah-62x120-127x59-cm) that has a back and neck support, you can set its’ parts how you get comfortable.

For your mouse and keyboard, which is the second most important part of a workspace, pick a pair of ergonomic mouse and keyboard. And you can support your wrists with a simple wrist support.

Ergonomics is not just buying tools and expensive/overpriced things, position of your body is the main thing. For your posture, you can do exercise and stand between study/work sessions.

A well-designed ergonomic setup can make a world of difference in your daily productivity. Consider the height and angle of your monitor as well. It should be at eye level to prevent strain on your neck and shoulders. A monitor stand or adjustable desk can help achieve this ideal positioning. Small adjustments, like using a footrest or an anti-fatigue mat if you use a standing desk, can also contribute significantly to your comfort.

Lighting is another critical aspect of an ergonomic workspace. Poor lighting can lead to eye strain and headaches, further diminishing your ability to focus. Natural light is ideal, but if that’s not possible, invest in a good desk lamp that provides adequate illumination without causing glare on your screen. Adjustable lighting options can help you customize the brightness to your preference, ensuring your eyes are not overworked.

Lastly, consider the layout of your workspace. Keep frequently used items within easy reach to avoid repetitive strain injuries from constant reaching or twisting. Decluttering your desk not only makes it easier to find things but also reduces distractions, helping you maintain a higher level of focus. By thoughtfully arranging your workspace and investing in ergonomic essentials, you create an environment that supports sustained productivity and well-being.

## Ambiance

If you are in comfort, you start to think about your environment, sounds, noise, motion seen from your window, etc. These are the kinds of things that will create a gap between you and your focus.

Creating the right ambiance in your workspace is key to maintaining concentration. Noise-canceling headphones or ambient music can help block out distracting sounds. Keeping your desk tidy minimizes visual distractions, and adjusting lighting to a comfortable level can reduce eye strain.

Consider the overall atmosphere as well. A few plants or personal items can enhance your workspace without causing distractions. By optimizing the ambiance, you create an environment that supports sustained focus and productivity.

In conclusion, the environment you create in your workspace has a profound impact on your productivity. By paying attention to ergonomics and ambiance, you can design a space that supports sustained focus and efficiency. Investing in ergonomic essentials and fine-tuning the visual and auditory elements of your workspace will help preserve your concentration, much like a well-designed thermos keeps ice from melting. With a thoughtful setup, you can significantly enhance your productivity and work experience, ensuring that you remain comfortable and focused throughout your day.
